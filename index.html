The Work of Formation

Saint Josemaría often said that the entire work of Opus Dei is summed up in providing formation. A formation that “does not concern only one part of the person, but the entire being: it has to reach equally the intellect, heart and will.”

And therefore, it is a human, professional, spiritual and apostolic formation, which aims at making the light and salt of Christ present in the ordinary activities of our life.

Recollections

Recollections are a chance to step back from the whirlwind of daily tasks for around 2 hours of quiet prayer spent looking at God, the world, and ourselves. It includes a spiritual reading, a meditation given by a priest, examination of conscience, a talk, and benediction. The most important thing is to put yourself in front of our Lord, to look at Him and to let Him look at you.

The Education Development Association (EDA)

The Education Development Association (EDA) is a registered charitable public company, limited by guarantee, and was established on 4 November 1964, setting out to empower young people to achieve their full potential through the delivery of educational programmes focused on character building.

EDA owns and operates a number of Study Centres around Australia. The spiritual formation and faith-based activities at its Centres are entrusted to Opus Dei. EDA now has study centres in NSW, QLD, VIC, and co-ordinates activities nationwide.

Aldridge Study Centre (QLD)
Aldridge Study Centre offers a wide range of educational activities tailored for the needs of high school boys, university students and for men who are already putting their talents at the service of their families and society.

Indulgences

Indulgences which may be obtained by Cooperators of the Prelature of the Holy Cross and Opus Dei.

I. Plenary Indulgence

Available under the usual conditions (sacramental confession, the reception of Holy Communion, prayer for the Roman Pontiff and his intentions) with their heart wholly detached from sin:

1) On the date of appointment as a Cooperator.

2) February 14, which in the Prelature is the feast of Holy Mary Mother of Fair Love, and also the anniversary of the beginning of the work of Opus Dei with women in 1930 and of the foundation of the Priestly Society of the Holy Cross in 1943.

3) March 19, solemnity of St. Joseph.

4) June 29, solemnity of the Apostles Sts. Peter and Paul.

5) September 14, feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross.

6) September 29, feast of Sts. Michael, Gabriel and Raphael, Archangels.

7) October 2, memorial (a solemnity in the Prelature) of the Holy Guardian Angels and the anniversary of the founding of Opus Dei.

8) December 27, feast of St. John, Apostle.

9) Whenever they attend a spiritual retreat which lasts at least three days.

10) At the hour of death, if during their life they have habitually prayed some prayers; or if, in that moment, they fulfill the conditions for a plenary indulgence.

II. Partial Indulgences

1) When they attend a Circle for Cooperators.

2) When they attend a day or evening of recollection.

3) Whenever they recite an aspiration while kissing the wooden Cross in the oratories of Opus Dei Centres.

4) Whenever they say a pious invocation during their work, be it manual or intellectual.

5) Whenever they say an aspiration while carrying out the works of piety or apostolate by which they cooperate in the spiritual work of the Prelature.

(Rescript No. 34/14 of April 28, 2014 & No. 17/75 of February 12, 1975 of the Apostolic Penitentiary; Enchiridion Indulgentiarum. Normae et concessiones, 1999.)
This is a translation of the original which is kept in our archives.
Rome, February 14, 2015 — Dr. Ernst Burkhart

Additional Spiritual Benefits

In addition, the Cooperators share in the spiritual benefits of the Prelature and, at their death, in the suffrages which Opus Dei offers for its deceased.
